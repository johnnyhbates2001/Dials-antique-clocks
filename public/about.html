Our story

Dials of Lymington

Dials Antique Clocks is a specialist dealer in fine antique clocks, based on Quay Hill in the Georgian market town of Lymington, on the edge of the New Forest.

We specialise in English and French clocks of the 18th and 19th centuries – from country longcase clocks and elegant bracket clocks to French four glass, carriage and skeleton clocks, along with marine chronometers and barometers.

Every clock we sell is carefully overhauled in our own workshop before it goes on sale, so you can buy with confidence. All clocks come with a twelve month guarantee, longcase clocks with eighteen months, and we can deliver anywhere in the world.

Repairs & restoration

Expert care for your clock

Whether it's a treasured family heirloom or a recent purchase, we can bring it back to reliable working order.

Movement overhauls

Complete dismantling, cleaning, re-bushing and repair of worn parts, then careful reassembly, oiling and regulation.

Cases & dials

Sympathetic restoration of wooden and brass cases, dials, hands and glass, preserving the character of the clock.

Collection & setting up

We can collect longcase clocks from your home and set them up again correctly once the work is complete.
