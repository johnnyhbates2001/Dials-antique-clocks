A clock shop on Quay Hill

We buy, sell and restore antique clocks from our shop on the cobbled hill between Lymington's High Street and the quay.

Our clocks

We specialise in English and French clocks from the 18th and 19th centuries: country longcases, bracket clocks, French four glass and carriage clocks, skeleton clocks and marine chronometers.

Every clock we sell is taken apart, cleaned and overhauled in our own workshop before it goes on sale, so it arrives running to time. Each one comes with a twelve month guarantee, or eighteen months for longcase clocks, and we can deliver anywhere in the world.

If you're looking for something in particular, call us. We often have clocks in the workshop that aren't listed yet.

Repairs and restoration

Whether it's a family heirloom that has stopped or a clock you've just bought, we can bring it back to reliable working order.

We work on movements, dials, hands and cases, and restore them sympathetically so the clock keeps its character.

Call 01590 673258

  1. Call or bring it in

    We look the clock over and give you a free estimate. We can collect longcase clocks from your home.

  2. Workshop overhaul

    The movement is taken apart, cleaned, worn parts repaired or replaced, then reassembled and regulated.

  3. Back home, guaranteed

    We test it running for several days, then return it and set it up again. The work is guaranteed.
